# Analytic proof route for V120-THE-02

Theorem 11A.2 of BQG v1.2 concerns local formal perturbative pure metric gravity. The statement uses analytic results, with the following steps for reproducing the argument.

1. In (S1)–(S2), put \(A=\omega(e)+C\). On the nondegenerate branch the contorsion equation is invertible and gives \(C=0\). Quotienting the local Lorentz frame yields the same Einstein–Hilbert term used in the canonical derivation. In BV, lift the change of variables canonically and eliminate the generalized auxiliary pair and the Lorentz contractible pair. The diffeomorphism BV action (S8) then obeys the classical master equation by gauge invariance and BRST nilpotence.
2. Work in the four-dimensional globally hyperbolic patch of §11A, with interior support and a smooth vacuum background. De Donder gauge fixing and the nonminimal pair give the normally hyperbolic free system used for the causal perturbation construction. Treat the fluctuating fields off shell. Allow the local covariant EFT counterterms.
3. At the first nonzero order of the renormalized Master Ward defect, the Ward consistency relation gives \(s a_L=0\bmod d\), where \(s\) is the classical BV differential including antifields. See [Fredenhagen–Rejzner, §4.3, Eqs. (85)–(88)](https://arxiv.org/html/1110.5232v3#S4.SS3).
4. In the pure Einstein case in four dimensions, the relevant ghost-number-one local anomaly cohomology in top form degree is trivial. For the cohomology, use [Barnich–Brandt–Henneaux, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9409104v1). For its use in the reduced metric theory, see [Brunetti–Fredenhagen–Rejzner, §3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/1306.1058v5#S3.SS4).
5. With \(a_L=s b_L+d c_L\), the local correction \(-\hbar^L\int b_L\) raises the order of the defect, as in (S9). The total derivative with a cutoff gives no contribution on the interior where the cutoff equals one. Repeat at each formal order to get (S10).

The derivation is an existence statement about local perturbative renormalization of a reduced metric EFT. Specific loop amplitudes, finite matching coefficients, the original lattice quantization and its selected BV measure, boundary anomalies, and convergence of the formal series are outside this statement.
