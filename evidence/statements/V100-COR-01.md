# V100-COR-01 — Massless spin two

**Evidence class:** `EXTERNAL_THEOREM_REDUCTION`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
Around $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+h_{\mu\nu}$, the quadratic gravity action is the massless Fierz--Pauli gauge class. After the linear constraints and gauge quotient, two physical helicities remain in four dimensions \cite{fierzpauli1939}.
```
