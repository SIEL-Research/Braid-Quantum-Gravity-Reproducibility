**Paper location:** Theorem 6.2, physical PDF page 6

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_CODE`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
The finite source action variation converges in the declared class to \begin{equation} \delta S_m =\frac12\int_M\sqrt{|g|}\,T^{\mu\nu}\delta g_{\mu\nu}\,d^4x. \label{eq:hilbert} \end{equation} Diffeomorphism variation gives the off-shell Noether identity and, on the matter equations of motion, \begin{equation} \nabla_\mu T^{\mu}{}_{\nu}=0. \label{eq:ward} \end{equation}
```
