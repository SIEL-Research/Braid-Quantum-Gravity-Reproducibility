# V100-THE-11 — Four-component finite Ward telescope

**Paper location:** Theorem 7.3, physical PDF page 7

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_PROOF`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
For every finite history length $n$ and all four components, \begin{equation} \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\Phi^k(D_\alpha) =S_\alpha-\Phi^n(S_\alpha). \label{eq:wardtelescope} \end{equation} This identity holds in all eight source sectors.
```

## What the evidence establishes

Algebraic Ward telescope conditional on D_alpha=S_alpha-Phi(S_alpha); physical selection is separate.
