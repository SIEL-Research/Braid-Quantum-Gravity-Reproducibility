## Statement in the paper

```latex
Let $A=H/\alpha$ with $\|A\|\le1$. The source-word LCU unitary, source dagger, signal qubit, and success-address reflection generate a walk $W$ satisfying \begin{equation} E^\dagger W^kE=T_k(A). \label{eq:cheb} \end{equation} For each of the 32 sector/control cases, the finite-spectrum interpolant \begin{equation} P_H(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}c_kT_k(x), \qquad P_H(\lambda_j/\alpha)=e^{-i\pi\lambda_j/3} \end{equation} gives an exact deterministic actuator using 203--39,102 $H$-block-encoding queries \cite{childswiebe2012,lowchuang2017,gilyen2019}.
```
