```latex
In the complete declared five-partition retraction class, the fixed event-conditioned five-label source selects one full matter corner with projector $P_{\rm src}$. Thus the rank-three summand supplies the matter routing and the signed rank-two complement supplies the finite Palatini geometry channel from the same source decomposition. No homogeneous-sphere identification, phase convention, coefficient fit, or target Einstein equation is used. We refer to $p_B$ as the Braid Routing Invariant (BRI) within this fixed source class.
```
