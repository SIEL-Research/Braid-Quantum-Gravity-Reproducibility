# V100-THE-34 — Variational Yukawa operator

**Paper location:** Theorem 14.2, physical PDF page 13

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_CODE`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
For the pointed affine source path, the exact variation $W(1)-W(0)$ equals $Y$. The forward/reverse CTP trilinears carry $Y$ and $Y^{\mathsf T}$, and the mixed Higgs variation produces the same positive $Y^{\mathsf T}Y$ entering the finite determinant. Thus the relative Yukawa operator is an action variation, not an independently fitted matrix.
```
