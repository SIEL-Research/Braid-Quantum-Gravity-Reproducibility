## Statement in the paper

```latex
Let $g_{AB,h}^n$, $A\le B$, be the ten source-soldered symmetric metric components. The normalized source update is \begin{equation} D_\tau^2 g_{AB,h}^n-\mathcal L_h(g_h^n)g_{AB,h}^n=N_{AB,h}^{\rm src}, \qquad \mathcal L_h(g)u=\frac1{25h^2}\sum_{\delta\in C_5^3\setminus\{0\}} c_\delta(g)\,[u(x+h\delta)-u(x)]. \label{eq:metric-euler-update} \end{equation} The factor $1/25$ is forced by the exact second moment $\sum_\delta c_\delta\delta_i\delta_j=50A_{ij}(g)$ and is not fitted. On every common smooth interval contained in the declared positive regular neighborhood, \begin{align} \|E_hI_hU-I_hE(U)\|_{H^{s-1}}&\le C_T(h^2+\tau^2),\\ \|B_hE_hI_hU\|_{H^{s-2}}&\le C_T(h+\tau^2/h),\\ \sup_{0\le t\le T}\|J_hU_h(t)-U(t)\|_{H^{s-1}} &\le C_T(\eta_h+h+\tau^2/h). \label{eq:metric-euler-bounds} \end{align} Thus $\tau=O(h)$ gives strong trajectory convergence and an $O(h)$ subsidiary-constraint defect. The actual inverse-metric principal tensor is recovered exactly in the limit for all ten components.
```
