## Statement in the paper

```latex
In the raw covariant Palatini parent, $e_0$ is the auxiliary multiplier for the source-derived scalar/vector constraints and $U_0$ is the temporal Lorentz gauge link. They are retained during variation, after which the constraints are imposed and a gauge is fixed or quotiented. On a local contractible regular branch, the marked source clock $\tau$ is a reachable gauge slice rather than an additional observable. In the source-derived canonical multiplier bundle, all eight spatial-orientation sectors have constraint rank four, zero discrete multiplier-gauge residual, a reachable source-clock representative and a four-dimensional reduced phase space. Independently, the literal raw six-face action has 24 nonzero alternating terms that split exactly into 12 $e_0e_iF_{jk}$ terms and 12 $e_ie_jF_{0k}(U_0)$ terms; its $e_0$ Hessian vanishes, its proper-Lorentz action is invariant, and two ordered noncommuting temporal links are jointly gauge-trivial on a contractible two-edge interval. Fixing or deleting $e_0,U_0$ before variation would instead remove the corresponding constraint equations.
```
