# V100-THE-41 — Refinement-invariant finite screen

**Paper location:** Theorem 17.1, physical PDF page 15

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_CODE`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
For $K$ complete source cells at refinement depth $m$, the composite count, area, and radius satisfy \begin{equation} N_m=K25^m,\qquad A_{\rm total}=K A_0,\qquad r_K=\sqrt K\,\ell_*. \label{eq:screen-refinement} \end{equation} Equivalently, \begin{equation} A_{\rm total}=\frac{45}{4}A_0 C_{{\rm exc},n}. \end{equation} The area is therefore invariant under complete $25$-adic descendant refinement.
```
