**Evidence class:** `EXTERNAL_THEOREM_REDUCTION`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
The injective source-event order excludes a periodic time quotient and selects the non-quotient universal cover of the repeated horizon gluing. On this cover all timelike and null geodesics are complete. A block time $T=n+\tau$ is a Cauchy time, so the spacetime is globally hyperbolic and has no Cauchy horizon. Global hyperbolicity together with timelike geodesic completeness then implies $C^0$-inextendibility, hence analytic inextendibility, by the Galloway--Ling--Sbierski theorem \cite{galloway2018}.
```
