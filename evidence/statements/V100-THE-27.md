# V100-THE-27 — Finite quantum BV completion

**Paper location:** Theorem 11.4, physical PDF page 11

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_CODE`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
On a simply connected, gap-admissible local regular branch, the pointed Hodge-polar construction defines a source-derived Ginsparg--Wilson regulator $D_n$ with index one and a rescaled spectral gap bounded below by one at every finite refinement depth. With the source half-density and the derived anomaly cancellation, the finite BV action $S_n^{\rm BV}$ satisfies \begin{equation} \frac12\{S_n^{\rm BV},S_n^{\rm BV}\}-i\hbar\Delta_nS_n^{\rm BV}=0. \label{eq:finite-qme} \end{equation} The determinant-inclusive Wilsonian BV pushforward from level $n+1$ to level $n$ preserves Eq.~\eqref{eq:finite-qme} exactly and composes at every finite depth. This is a finite-cutoff quantum closure, not yet a construction of the infinite-depth continuum measure \cite{ginspargwilson1982,batalinvilkovisky1981,cattaneomnevreshetikhin2012}.
```

## What the evidence establishes

Finite-depth QME and BV pushforward on simply connected gap-admissible regular branches; no continuum measure.
