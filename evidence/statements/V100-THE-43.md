# V100-THE-43 — Static regular black-bounce

**Paper location:** Theorem 18.1, physical PDF page 16

**Evidence class:** `PUBLIC_CODE`

## Statement in the paper

```latex
In the declared static spherical six-face Palatini class, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:spherical-palatini}--\eqref{eq:black-bounce-centering} have the exact stationary solution \begin{equation} R(X)=\sqrt{X^2+\ell_*^2},\qquad F(X)=1-\frac{r_h}{R(X)},\qquad X\in\mathbb R. \label{eq:black-bounce} \end{equation} It has two asymptotically flat ends, $R\ge\ell_*>0$, bounded curvature and a regular ingoing/outgoing Eddington--Finkelstein and Kruskal atlas across every horizon.
```
