## Bold hypothesis

- **Interpretive leap:** physical sufficiency of the neutral carrier should be
  decided by its source-generated observable algebra, not by an isomorphism to
  a previously named external three-copy module.
- **New representation criterion:** the operational carrier is internally
  complete if the exact algebra generated by the three source reflections and
  `C_g` is `M3(C)` with scalar commutant.
- **Gap mechanism:** place each frozen branch weight on its actual source

- **Source status:** both objects are derived from BQGNEUT-022 branch typing,
  source conjugation and frozen weights.  Identifying `L_w` with the physical
  neutrino Hamiltonian is not derived and is not assumed here.

## Strongest ordinary alternatives

1. Every qutrit with a diagonal algebra and one shift generates `M3`; algebraic

2. A weighted real graph Laplacian can split eigenvalues but has a real
   eigenframe and therefore zero CP invariant.  Gap splitting and the BQGNEUT-
   022 complex phase frame may fail to close simultaneously.

## Exact falsifiers

- External-carrier removal fails if the generated algebra has dimension below
  nine or a nonscalar commutant.
- Gap splitting fails if `L_w` has a repeated eigenvalue.
- Joint physical PMNS closure fails if the same derived propagation operator
  does not retain nonzero CP, physical matter typing and an absolute scale.

## Minimum decisive test and stopping rule

Use exact rational closure of the generator algebra, exact commutant rank, and
the exact characteristic polynomial of `L_w`.  Stop without parameter scans,
PMNS/NuFIT values, logarithm branches or physical-scale fitting.

## Claim ceiling

At most an internally complete source-generated neutral qutrit algebra and a
source-weighted nondegenerate dimensionless Laplacian spectrum.  This does not
derive the Standard-Model lepton embedding, a single CP-violating massive
propagation operator, an absolute neutrino scale, empirical PMNS agreement or
completed quantum gravity.
