## Frozen construction

counting/BKM-positive Hodge--Dirac operator and let `Gamma_n=(-1)^degree`.
Let `P_h,n` be the projector onto the unique harmonic algebra unit. Define

`S_n = B_n |B_n|^{-1}` on `ker(B_n)^perp`, and `S_n=+1` on `P_h,n`,

where the plus sign is fixed by unital pointing. Set

`V_n=Gamma_n S_n`, `D_GW,n=1-V_n`.

No Wilson parameter, mass threshold, measured fermion datum or target index is
used.
