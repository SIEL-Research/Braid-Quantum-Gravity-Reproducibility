# DPA-SCOUT-BGCE466-001

## Question

Can the already source-selected BGCE362 stochastic `G1/H_i` cycle be transported, without fitted pulses, to the BGCE458-464 finite complex carrier as a nontrivial quantum engine?

## Frozen gates

1. Reconstruct the 36-complex-dimensional carrier, its source `Z2` grading, and all eight signed sectors from hash-pinned inputs.
2. Reconstruct `G1,H_1,H_2,H_3` from the actual source. No observable-to-control promotion beyond this already-derived BGCE126/362 family is allowed.
3. Audit the direct 125-dimensional controls first. Any failure to preserve the BGCE458 carrier must be disclosed and cannot be hidden.
4. Apply only the canonical source-counting orthogonal compression `O -> B* O B`, where the columns of `B` are the normalized `+i` source-cycle eigenvectors. This is the unique carrier compression in the fixed counting/cup inner product. It is an explicit exploratory interpretive leap, not a claim that the original 125-dimensional pulse preserves the carrier.
5. In every signed sector and both `Z2` blocks, require four Hermitian compressed generators, exact grading preservation, a nonzero `G1-H_i` commutator, and six pairwise projectively distinct `pi/3` order unitaries.
6. Push the BGCE362 uniform law on the six total orders through those unitaries. Require a CPTP mixed-unitary channel and Kraus/Choi rank six in every one of the 16 blocks.

## Decision rule

- `SCOPED PASS`: all gates pass, yielding a finite source-scheduled stochastic quantum-engine candidate under the canonical carrier-compression hypothesis.
- `NO-GO`: any block becomes commuting/trivial, any order collapses up to phase, positivity/trace preservation fails, or an unfrozen pulse, coefficient, or schedule is needed.

The gate cannot establish a physical actuator, seconds calibration, deterministic label-to-order map, universal `U(18)xU(18)` controllability, continuum/QFT completion, or empirical confirmation. MMR/CGR, a target Einstein equation, Einstein-Hilbert/Fierz-Pauli actions, manual `3/5`, and coefficient fitting are forbidden.
